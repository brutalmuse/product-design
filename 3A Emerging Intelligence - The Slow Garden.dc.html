The Slow Garden

An Interactive Slow Living Landing Page

Role: Product Designer Year: 2026 Type: Experimental Web Experience, Concept UI/UX Design
Open prototype in new tab ↗

Overview

The Slow Garden is a desktop landing page concept designed around the philosophy of slow living. Set against a serene summer garden landscape, the interface uses a foggy glass interaction where users can clear the mist to gradually reveal the view beyond. The experience transforms a simple landing screen into a quiet digital ritual, encouraging pause, presence, and emotional softness.

Through atmospheric visuals, minimal navigation, delicate typography, and subtle interaction cues, the project explores how digital interfaces can feel sensory, poetic, and restorative — rather than fast, functional, or overwhelming.

Focus

My focus was on translating the idea of slow living into an immersive digital experience through mood, motion, typography, glass-morphism, and cursor-led interaction.
