Overview

Feral Type is an experimental display typeface shaped by wavy contours, bold weight, and raw organic energy. Designed for expressive headlines and visual statements, it brings a sense of movement, attitude, and playful distortion to posters, editorial layouts, music graphics, and digital culture projects. Its sculptural letterforms make every word feel loud, alive, and instinctive.
