About

Shruthi FHEA

01

WHAT DRAWS ME

I’m drawn to ideas that make technology feel more intuitive, expressive, and human. I enjoy exploring the small behaviours and emotional details that shape how people experience digital products.

02

MY JOURNEY

I began with an engineering mindset and gradually moved towards design, research, and education. I still carry that systems-based way of thinking, but now pair it with visual experimentation and human observation.

04

THESE DAYS

I divide my time between designing, experimenting, teaching, and mentoring. This allows my professional practice and academic work to continually challenge and inform one another.

03

MY APPROACH

I do not begin by asking what the interface should look like. I begin by asking what a person should understand, feel, or be able to do.

05

INSPIRATION

I collect ideas from people, culture, technology, cinema, editorial design, movement, sound, and the strange behaviours hidden inside everyday life.
