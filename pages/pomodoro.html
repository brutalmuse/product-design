Focus Ritual

A working Pomodoro timer built to feel calm and tactile — a split-screen companion for focus sessions and short, restorative breaks.

Role: Product Designer Year: 2026 Type: Interactive Prototype

Overview

Focus Ritual pairs a Pomodoro timer with a lightweight task board, split across one uncluttered screen. The left panel holds hand-written-feel sticky notes for the session's tasks; the right panel holds a spinning vinyl-style timer with a focus colour the visitor picks themselves — the colour carries through both panels the moment it's chosen.

When a break starts, the whole screen hands off to a freshly generated maze — a small, deliberate detour from the desk, navigable with arrow keys and skippable at any time.

Focus

Designing a focus tool that feels personal from the very first click — before the timer even starts — rather than a blank utility waiting for input.

Try It — Live Prototype

Pick a focus colour, add a few tasks, and press Start. When the break kicks in, use the arrow keys to solve the maze.
