Overview

I was commissioned to redesign the Fashiorama website, which focuses on empowering women by providing personalized fashion and wellness recommendations tailored to their unique body shapes. The website is designed to boost confidence, refine personal style, and prioritize wellness, while fostering a supportive community. The previous design was outdated, cluttered, and lacked a user-friendly interface, which hindered user engagement and overall functionality.

Problem

The original Fashiorama website suffered from poor user experience, confusing navigation, lack of focus on personalization, and an unengaging community platform. These issues resulted in high bounce rates and a disconnect between the brand's mission and the user experience.

Solution

Through a complete redesign, I enhanced the user experience by creating a streamlined, confidence-boosting interface that emphasizes personalization, community, and wellness — transforming the site into an engaging, supportive platform where users can discover their unique style and feel empowered.

Style Guide

Typography Fashiorama Lora Italic for headlines and moments of warmth; Helvetica for body copy, labels, and UI — a quiet, confident pairing.

Imagery

Bold, maximalist fashion editorial — real bodies of every shape shot against saturated, surreal backdrops. Confident poses, playful props, and vivid color grounded by the site's minimal black-and-white UI, so the personality lives in the photography, not the chrome.

Grid System

A fixed 200px label column paired with a fluid content column structures every case-study section. Product screens sit on a 1200px browser canvas that scales down proportionally, and feed/pillar content runs on a 3-column grid with consistent gutters.
