Editorial Workflow

A structured workflow for faculty to submit bilingual newsletter stories, and for the designer to see which content is final.

Role: Product Designer Year: 2026 Type: Internal Proposal
A workflow proposal for SoCCI, BUV Proposed internally. Not yet built. Try the prototype ↓

Observation

I am the designer and publisher of the SoCCI quarterly newsletter at British University Vietnam. For each issue, faculty submit their stories in a shared Excel sheet, with English in one column and Vietnamese in the next.

While preparing the layout for Issue 3, I could not confirm which stories were final. Several stories had changed since my last check. The only way to find the changes was to compare each paragraph with the layout.

The planner showed further gaps:

  • —Most cells in the Link / Source column show only the label “Photo folder”. The label does not show which folder opens or which photos to use.
  • —Instructions are written inside link cells, for example “Please pick 3-4 photos”.
  • —One cell contains a Facebook URL and a long SharePoint guest link as plain text, with a request to add a QR code.
  • —One story has no images. This is recorded in the link column as “No image for this. Just text”.
  • —Only 1 of 11 stories has a status, and none has an owner.

To address this, I proposed a structured workflow to the team.

The Problem

The spreadsheet works as a schedule. It does not support writing or handover.

  • —Each story, including its headline and body, is entered in a single cell.
  • —The English and Vietnamese versions are not checked against each other.
  • —Status, deadline and owner fields are mostly empty.
  • —Requests are recorded in Notes without an assigned owner.
  • —Links are labelled only “Photo folder” or pasted as plain URLs, with instructions mixed in.
  • —The sheet has no clear hierarchy. It does not show which information is most important or which story comes first.
  • —There is no step to confirm that a story is final.
  • —Changes to a story are not communicated to anyone.

Design Opportunity

How might faculty submit final stories in two languages, so the designer can see which content is final and what has changed?

Goals

01

Provide a dedicated writing space

Faculty write English and Vietnamese content in separate fields. A checklist shows which required information is missing.

02

Make confirmation explicit

A story is final only when its contributor confirms it. Completed fields alone do not make a story final.

03

Show every change

When a contributor edits a confirmed story, the designer sees the exact changes and can copy the updated text into the layout.

Try it

The two prototypes below show each side of the handover. They share the same data. When you confirm a story in the Faculty workspace, it appears in the Editorial workspace.
